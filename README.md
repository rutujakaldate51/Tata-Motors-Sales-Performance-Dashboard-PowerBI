🚗 Tata Motors Sales Performance Dashboard
📌 Project Overview

The Tata Motors Sales Performance Dashboard is an interactive Power BI project developed to analyze sales performance, revenue trends, customer insights, dealer performance, and vehicle preferences across India.

Tata Motors Sales Performance Dashboard – Project Explanation

Hello everyone!

Today, I'd like to present my Tata Motors Sales Performance Dashboard, which I developed in Power BI to analyze sales, revenue, customer behavior, and dealer performance.

The goal of this dashboard is to help management monitor business performance and make data-driven decisions using interactive visualizations.

Dashboard Overview

At the top of the dashboard, I have included the main KPIs that provide a quick business summary.
Total Cars Sold shows the total number of vehicles sold.
Top Selling Model identifies the best-performing Tata car.
Total Revenue displays the total revenue generated.
Total Customers shows the total customer count.
Average Selling Price provides the average selling price of vehicles.

These KPIs allow users to understand the overall business performance at a glance.

Interactive Filters
On the left side, I have added slicers that allow users to filter the dashboard dynamically.
Users can filter by:

Car Model
Fuel Type
Month
Year
Transmission
Dealer

Additionally, State and City filters are available at the top of the dashboard.
As the filters change, every visual updates automatically.

Revenue & Profit Trend
This visual compares yearly revenue with profit percentage.
It helps identify whether revenue and profitability are improving over time and supports long-term business planning.

Revenue by Year
This chart displays revenue generated each year.
Management can easily compare annual business performance and identify growth trends.

Sales by State
The map visual displays vehicle sales across different Indian states.
This helps identify high-performing regions and areas with growth opportunities.

Monthly Sales Trend
The line chart shows monthly sales performance.
It helps identify seasonal demand patterns and peak sales months.

Sales by Car Model
This bar chart compares sales across different Tata car models.
It helps determine which models perform well and which may need additional marketing efforts.

Top Dealers by Sales
This visual highlights the highest-performing dealerships based on sales.
It helps management recognize top dealers and improve dealership performance.

Customer Rating
The rating card displays the average customer satisfaction score.
This metric helps evaluate customer experience and service quality.

Fuel Type Analysis
This chart compares sales across different fuel types such as:
Petrol
Diesel
CNG
EV
It helps understand changing customer preferences toward electric vehicles and other fuel options.

Customer Age Group
This treemap shows which customer age groups purchase the most vehicles.
It provides valuable insights for targeted marketing campaigns.

Body Type Analysis
This donut chart shows the popularity of different vehicle body types.
It helps understand customer preferences for SUVs, Hatchbacks, and Sedans.

Transmission Analysis
This visual compares Manual and Automatic transmission sales.
It helps identify customer buying preferences and market demand.

Key Insights

From this dashboard, we can identify:
The highest-selling Tata model.
The model generating the highest revenue.
Top-performing dealers.
State-wise sales performance.
Customer age group preferences.
Fuel type demand.
Body type popularity.
Monthly and yearly sales trends.
